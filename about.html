About

A little about the person behind this blog

Alex

Living with low vision · Advocate · Helper

Hi, I’m Alex. A few years ago I began losing part of my vision. What started as a medical challenge slowly became the reason I now dedicate most of my time to helping people who face the same reality.

This blog is a place where I share practical advice, honest stories, and the tools that helped me keep living fully. I write for anyone with low vision or visual impairment — and for the people who support them.

My story in short

After the diagnosis I spent months feeling lost. Then I discovered accessibility tools, support communities, and the simple truth that many of us are walking similar paths. Once I found my footing, I decided I didn’t want others to feel as alone as I once did.

What I do now

  • Share everyday tips for living with low vision
  • Recommend accessible technology and simple home adjustments
  • Write about the emotional side of vision loss
  • Connect people so no one has to figure everything out alone

What you’ll find here

Most posts fall into three areas:

  • Personal Journey — honest stories about adaptation and finding purpose
  • Practical Tips — tools, apps, and small changes that make daily life easier
  • Community — the power of supporting each other and building connection

Get in touch

You can reach me at hello@beyondsight.blog. I’m always happy to hear from others living with low vision or from people who want to help.
